Our Story

Two friends. Two worlds. One cup.

Mohamed grew up in Tunisia, where coffee is a ritual — a reason to slow down, a language spoken between friends. When he arrived in Vietnam and discovered the misty highland farms of Da Lat, something clicked. The care, the patience, the pride of the farmers reminded him of home.

Daniel grew up in Hong Kong, where the city moves fast and the coffee culture has grown into something world-class. When he came to visit Mohamed in Vietnam, the slow mornings and highland air got under his skin.

On one of those mornings, somewhere between a second cup and a third, we asked each other: why isn't this coffee in Hong Kong yet? We didn't have a good answer. So we decided to do something about it.

Mohamed · Da Lat, Vietnam

"We visit the farms ourselves, sit with the farmers, and learn the story behind every lot before we ever bring it to Hong Kong."

Where We Source

Four countries. One standard.

Every origin we carry was chosen for a reason. Not the most coffee — the best. Each one selected by hand, brought here with care.

01

Vietnam

Da Lat Highlands

At 1,500 metres, volcanic soil and highland mist slow the ripening of the cherry — and that patience shows in the cup. Stone fruit, dark chocolate, lingering sweetness. This is where our story started.

02

Cambodia

Mondulkiri Province

One of the world's least-known coffee origins, and one of its most exciting. Small farms producing arabica of rare clarity, grown in ancient highland forests where altitude and shade work together.

03

Indonesia

Sumatra · Java · Flores

Indonesia's archipelago produces coffees as varied as its landscape — from the earthy depth of Sumatra to the bright, clean profiles of Flores. Centuries of cultivation, a thousand expressions.

04

Philippines

Benguet · Sagada

High in the Cordillera mountains, Filipino specialty coffee is quietly rewriting what the region can produce. Floral, nuanced, and largely undiscovered by the world — until now.
